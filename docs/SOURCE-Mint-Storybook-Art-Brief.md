# Art Brief: *Mint's Perfect Present* — 7 storybook illustrations

This document is the complete and only specification for a set of seven
illustrations. Every instruction in it is mandatory. Where it describes the
character, the style or the rule about writing, those descriptions must be
reproduced exactly and must not be shortened, paraphrased or varied between
images.

The seven images belong to one storybook and must look as though one illustrator
drew them in one sitting, on the same afternoon, with the same pencils.

---

## 1. THE CHARACTER — Mint

The same girl appears in every one of the seven images. She is called Mint. Her
appearance never changes between images. Her clothes never change between
images. Her age never changes between images.

**Mint is:** a 13-year-old Thai schoolgirl, small and slim, who clearly looks
thirteen years old and never older. She has long straight black hair worn
completely loose and untied, falling past her shoulders to the middle of her
back, parted in the centre, with a soft fringe across her forehead. Her hair is
never tied back, never in a ponytail, never in a bun, and never held by a bow,
a band or a clip. She wears round glasses with thin gold-toned metal frames and
clear lenses, and she wears them in every image. She has a round face with soft
cheeks, warm brown almond-shaped eyes, a small nose and light golden-tan skin,
and one small dark mole just below the left corner of her mouth.

**Mint wears,** in all seven images: a crisp plain white short-sleeved school
blouse with nothing printed, embroidered, badged, labelled or written on it; a
navy blue knee-length pleated skirt; short white socks; plain black school
shoes; and a tan leather satchel backpack worn on both shoulders. She wears no
jewellery, no watch, no make-up and no nail varnish.

**The three identifying marks** that prove it is the same girl are her loose
untied long hair, her round glasses, and the small mole below the left corner
of her mouth. All three must be visible in every image.

**Mint is never** an adult, never a teenager of sixteen or older, never a young
woman, never dressed in ordinary clothes, and never replaced by a different
person. In every image where a customer, a shopper or a schoolgirl appears, that
person is Mint.

## 2. SUPPORTING CHARACTERS

**The shop assistant** appears in scenes 5 and 6 only. She is a friendly Thai
woman in her late twenties with black hair in a low ponytail, wearing a plain
dark green apron with nothing written on it over a plain grey t-shirt. She is
the same woman in both scenes.

**Nok, the classmate,** appears in scene 7 only. She is a Thai schoolgirl of the
same age as Mint, wearing the identical white blouse and navy pleated skirt, but
she is noticeably taller than Mint, she has long black hair tied up in a high
ponytail with a plain navy band, she wears no glasses, and she has a longer,
narrower face. Nok and Mint must be easy to tell apart at a glance: Mint's hair
is loose and she wears glasses, Nok's hair is tied up and she does not.

No other people appear in close focus in any image.

## 3. ART STYLE

Every image is a warm coloured pencil and soft watercolour children's storybook
illustration with clean ink linework and gentle cel shading.

It is never photo-realistic, never a photograph, never a 3D render, never anime
or manga, never a flat vector infographic and never a diagram. There are no
sparkle effects, no speed lines and no emotion symbols floating in the air.

Lighting is soft and even with no harsh shadows. Each image is a single
continuous scene in landscape format, roughly four units wide to three units
tall. The face of every character is fully visible and is never cut off by the
edge of the frame.

## 4. THE RULE ABOUT WRITING

No image may contain any legible writing of any kind.

This means no words, no letters, no numbers, no signs, no shop names, no brand
names, no logos, no labels, no captions, no arrows carrying text, no speech
bubbles, no price tags with figures, no printed receipts, no text on any screen,
no watermarks and no signature.

Every surface that would normally carry writing must be left blank, or filled
with abstract shapes and marks that are clearly not letterforms. Posters are
plain blocks of colour. Screens are a soft even glow. Receipts are blank paper
with faint grey horizontal lines only. Banknotes are plain coloured paper with a
faint abstract pattern and no figures, portraits or words. Coins carry a soft
abstract relief pattern with no numerals or inscriptions.

An image containing any readable character has failed and must be redrawn.

## 5. THE SEVEN SCENES

Draw exactly these seven, in this order, one image each.

### Scene 1 — Mint decides to save

Warm cream and honey palette, late afternoon.

Mint is the only person in the image. She is a 13-year-old Thai schoolgirl,
small and slim, who clearly looks thirteen and never older, with long straight
black hair worn completely loose and untied falling to the middle of her back
with a soft fringe, round glasses with thin gold-toned metal frames, a round
face with soft cheeks, warm brown almond-shaped eyes, light golden-tan skin, and
one small dark mole just below the left corner of her mouth. She wears a crisp
plain white short-sleeved school blouse with nothing written on it, a navy blue
knee-length pleated skirt, short white socks and plain black school shoes.

She sits at a wooden desk in her bedroom, leaning forward with quiet
determination, pressing a coin carefully into the slot of a pink ceramic piggy
bank. Pushed away to the far edge of the desk, out of her reach, sit a tall
plastic cup of bubble tea with a wide straw and a white games controller. A few
more coins lie beside an open notebook. Her tan leather satchel backpack hangs
on the back of her chair.

No writing anywhere. The notebook page is blank and the piggy bank is plain.

### Scene 2 — The cash machine and the hidden charge

Cool teal and pale blue palette, lit by the glow of the machine.

Mint is the only person in focus. She is a 13-year-old Thai schoolgirl, small
and slim, who clearly looks thirteen and never older, with long straight black
hair worn completely loose and untied falling to the middle of her back with a
soft fringe, round glasses with thin gold-toned metal frames, a round face with
soft cheeks, warm brown almond-shaped eyes, light golden-tan skin, and one small
dark mole just below the left corner of her mouth. She wears a crisp plain white
short-sleeved school blouse with nothing written on it, a navy blue knee-length
pleated skirt, short white socks, plain black school shoes, and a tan leather
satchel backpack on both shoulders.

She stands at a cash machine set into a wall inside a shopping mall, seen from
her side. She has just taken banknotes from the slot and holds them in one hand,
and she frowns slightly at the machine's screen with her eyebrows drawn
together, disappointed rather than angry. A few blurred shoppers pass far
behind her.

No writing anywhere. The screen is a soft even blue glow with no text, numbers
or interface. The keypad buttons are blank. The banknotes are plain coloured
paper with no figures or words.

### Scene 3 — The phone case that costs too much

Bright, slightly pink retail palette with soft highlights.

Mint is the only person in focus. She is a 13-year-old Thai schoolgirl, small
and slim, who clearly looks thirteen and never older, with long straight black
hair worn completely loose and untied falling to the middle of her back with a
soft fringe, round glasses with thin gold-toned metal frames, a round face with
soft cheeks, warm brown almond-shaped eyes, light golden-tan skin, and one small
dark mole just below the left corner of her mouth. She wears a crisp plain white
short-sleeved school blouse with nothing written on it, a navy blue knee-length
pleated skirt, short white socks, plain black school shoes, and a tan leather
satchel backpack on both shoulders.

She stands in front of a glass display stand in a smart mall shop, holding a
glittery phone case in both hands and looking down at it with wide eyes and
slightly raised eyebrows, shocked at how much it costs. A row of other decorated
phone cases sits on the stand beside it. Her free hand is pressed lightly to her
cheek.

No writing anywhere. The phone cases carry only patterns, glitter and plain
colour, never a brand mark, a fruit shape, a badge or a name. The shop behind
her carries no signs and no price tickets.

### Scene 4 — The paint set in the sale

Warm pastel palette with soft yellows and pinks.

Mint is the only person in the image. She is a 13-year-old Thai schoolgirl,
small and slim, who clearly looks thirteen and never older, with long straight
black hair worn completely loose and untied falling to the middle of her back
with a soft fringe, round glasses with thin gold-toned metal frames, a round
face with soft cheeks, warm brown almond-shaped eyes, light golden-tan skin, and
one small dark mole just below the left corner of her mouth. She wears a crisp
plain white short-sleeved school blouse with nothing written on it, a navy blue
knee-length pleated skirt, short white socks, plain black school shoes, and a
tan leather satchel backpack on both shoulders.

She stands in a bright stationery shop, delighted, holding up an open
rectangular watercolour paint set at chest height so the rows of pastel paint
pans face the viewer. Shelves of notebooks and pots of coloured pencils fill the
wall behind her. Beside her stands a tall shop sign board on a stand, drawn as a
bold pink and yellow starburst shape.

No writing anywhere. The sign board is a plain coloured starburst with no words,
letters, percentages or numbers at all. The notebooks on the shelves have blank
covers.

### Scene 5 — The broken card machine

Cool, calm palette with warm lamplight over the counter.

Exactly two people appear and no others.

On the left, the customer is Mint: a 13-year-old Thai schoolgirl, small and
slim, who clearly looks thirteen and never older, with long straight black hair
worn completely loose and untied falling to the middle of her back with a soft
fringe, round glasses with thin gold-toned metal frames, a round face with soft
cheeks, warm brown almond-shaped eyes, light golden-tan skin, and one small dark
mole just below the left corner of her mouth. She wears a crisp plain white
short-sleeved school blouse with nothing written on it, a navy blue knee-length
pleated skirt, short white socks, plain black school shoes, and a tan leather
satchel backpack on both shoulders. The customer is a schoolgirl, never an adult
woman and never a person in ordinary clothes.

On the right, behind the counter, stands a friendly Thai woman in her late
twenties with black hair in a low ponytail, wearing a plain dark green apron with
nothing written on it over a plain grey t-shirt.

Mint holds out a folded banknote across the shop counter towards the assistant,
who reaches to take it and smiles apologetically. Between them on the counter
sits a small card payment terminal, dark and switched off, with a plain
rectangle of paper taped over its screen. A wrapped parcel waits at the
assistant's elbow.

No writing anywhere. The paper taped over the terminal is blank, the terminal
screen is dark and empty, its buttons blank, and the banknote is plain coloured
paper with no figures or words.

### Scene 6 — The bag, the receipt and the change

Warm honey palette, cosy shop lighting.

Exactly two people appear and no others, and they are the same two people as in
the previous scene.

The customer is Mint: a 13-year-old Thai schoolgirl, small and slim, who clearly
looks thirteen and never older, with long straight black hair worn completely
loose and untied falling to the middle of her back with a soft fringe, round
glasses with thin gold-toned metal frames, a round face with soft cheeks, warm
brown almond-shaped eyes, light golden-tan skin, and one small dark mole just
below the left corner of her mouth. She wears a crisp plain white short-sleeved
school blouse with nothing written on it, a navy blue knee-length pleated skirt,
short white socks, plain black school shoes, and a tan leather satchel backpack
on both shoulders.

Behind the counter stands the same friendly Thai woman in her late twenties with
black hair in a low ponytail, in a plain dark green apron over a plain grey
t-shirt.

The assistant hands over a brown paper carrier bag with one hand and a long
narrow strip of till receipt paper with the other. Mint reaches out to take the
bag, smiling. On the counter between them lie four or five gold and silver
coins, one of them larger than the rest and catching the light.

No writing anywhere. The receipt is blank white paper with only faint grey
horizontal lines suggesting print, and no letters, numbers, barcode or header.
The paper bag is plain brown. The coins carry only a soft abstract relief
pattern.

### Scene 7 — Nok chips in for the cake

Bright, warm bakery palette with soft pinks and creams.

Exactly two people appear and no others, and both are schoolgirls of the same
age in the same uniform.

On the left is Mint: a 13-year-old Thai schoolgirl, small and slim, who clearly
looks thirteen and never older, with long straight black hair worn completely
loose and untied falling to the middle of her back with a soft fringe, round
glasses with thin gold-toned metal frames, a round face with soft cheeks, warm
brown almond-shaped eyes, light golden-tan skin, and one small dark mole just
below the left corner of her mouth. She wears a crisp plain white short-sleeved
school blouse with nothing written on it, a navy blue knee-length pleated skirt,
short white socks, plain black school shoes, and a tan leather satchel backpack.
Her hands are clasped together near her chin in relief and gratitude.

On the right is her classmate Nok, a Thai schoolgirl the same age in the
identical white blouse and navy pleated skirt, but noticeably taller than Mint,
with long black hair tied up in a high ponytail with a plain navy band, no
glasses, and a longer, narrower face. She smiles and holds out two folded
banknotes over the counter.

They stand together at a glass bakery counter. Inside the glass, on a white
stand, sits a round cream cake topped with fresh strawberries. Croissants and
bread rolls fill the shelves behind.

No writing anywhere. The banknotes are plain coloured paper with no figures or
words. The bakery has no signs, no menu boards and no price cards in the
display.
